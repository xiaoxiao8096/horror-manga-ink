# Environment-First Ink v5

Use this reference before every portrait or street conversion. The goal is an original black-ink psychological-horror image in which a normal setting becomes slightly uncomfortable through uneven material observation—not an admired, polished action-comic hero portrait.

## Invert the Hierarchy

Treat the person as part of the real location. Preserve face, hair, glasses, clothing, and lens perspective, but do not spotlight, symmetrize, beautify, or make the face the only impressive object. Let the actual wall, shutter, road, vehicle edge, lamp pole, tree, cable, doorway, and recess share visual weight.

## Observe Materials Locally

Use short, broken marks that match existing material: worn wall/shutter edges, irregular pavement seams, interrupted vehicle reflections, tangled foliage, lamp-pole weathering, and dark recess boundaries. Put this detail only where the source supports it. Do not apply a global hatch, uniform grain, dot overlay, decorative splatter, or clean pattern.

## Keep the Portrait Unglamorous

Preserve the source camera's awkwardness. Keep natural eye scale, facial asymmetry, glasses pads/rims, hair roots, flyaways, under-eye planes, nose, and mouth corners factual. Use small irregular line clusters at real transitions rather than long elegant face-contour hatching. Break hair into uneven source-following clumps; do not use a smooth shiny black hair helmet.

## Allocate Black and White

Use black for genuine recesses, shirt interiors, selected hair clumps, and source-supported cast shadows. Preserve quiet pale/white environment fields. Allow small dense detail islands to interrupt those fields. Do not make the face a cleanly lit central poster subject or use cinematic rim light.

## Ordinary Discomfort

Let mundane details become over-observed: the lamp, road, shutter, tree, glasses reflection, vehicle edge, wall stain, or doorway. The scene must remain normal. Do not add an event, a monster, injury, body change, another person, or a new setting.

## Anti-Action-Manga Block

Avoid heroic central portrait emphasis, polished anatomy, elegant long contour hatching, flattering symmetry, dramatic action shadows, speed-line energy, smooth black hair masses, visual coolness, poster-like contrast, decorative screentone, neon lighting, and glamourized face modeling.

# Monochrome Psychological-Horror Ink Prompt Templates v3

Use the source photo as the visual reference. Preserve its original aspect ratio and composition unless the user explicitly asks for a crop. Create an original black-ink psychological-horror still; do not emulate a named living artist, a copyrighted character, a published panel, or a specific comic.

## Shared preservation block

```text
Preserve the source photo's recognizable subject identity, facial structure, expression, pose, clothing silhouette, camera angle, lens perspective, composition, major object placement, background architecture, and all spatial relationships. Keep the ordinary real-world setting factual and readable. Do not add people, dialogue, readable text, injuries, gore, body distortion, new narrative events, copyrighted characters, or symbols.
```

## Default medium-intensity prompt

```text
Transform the provided photo into an original monochrome psychological-horror manga still. Keep the source world ordinary, factual, and readable. Render with black ink on broad paper-white fields: use pure black only for real structural masses, such as hair, clothing interiors, deep recesses, and source-supported shadow cores. Create gray only with sparse, precise directional hatching and short line clusters that follow actual form; do not use a full-image screentone or gray wash. Allocate fine variable-weight pen detail to one or two existing narrative anchors, such as a held gaze, glasses reflection, eyelid crease, hairline, narrow recess, practical lamp, or nearby architectural edge. Keep the rest simplified through white paper, clean silhouettes, and quiet negative space. The tension must come from an ordinary scene observed too closely, not from added horror content.

[Shared preservation block]

Avoid all color, halftone blankets, dot overlays, all-over hatching, gray airbrush, generic film grain, decorative ink splatter, glossy oversized eyes, blush, cute/chibi anime drift, smooth cel shading, clean vector fills, painterly rendering, neon rim light, speech bubbles, captions, logos, gore, extra anatomy, beauty retouching, facial drift, warped perspective, or source-composition drift.
```

## Subtle prompt modifier

```text
Keep broad white breathing room and minimal black structural masses. Use only a few localized pen details and hatching clusters around the selected tension anchor. The result should remain almost calmly ordinary at first glance.
```

## Intense prompt modifier

```text
Strengthen only the imbalance between normal reality and the selected tension anchor: make structural black shapes more decisive, concentrate fine pen detail more tightly, and let quiet white fields feel more exposed. Do not add events, distort anatomy, or cover the page with darkness or texture.
```
